Transitioning from structured master's coursework to open-ended doctoral research demands a profound shift in daily workflow and intellectual focus. 

In coursework, milestones and metrics are predefined; in research, defining the right questions and navigating uncertainty becomes the primary task.

### Building a Continuous Research Workflow

Over the past months, my research environment has evolved towards a tighter loop between literature ingestion, note distillation, and automated synthesis:

1. **Second Brain Repository**: Maintaining structured markdown notes linking concepts, tapeout errata, and schematic variations.
2. **Autonomous Tooling**: Utilizing custom scripts and AI research agents to triage literature, summarize benchmarks, and trace citation graphs.
3. **Public Output**: Treating this personal site as a filtered window into synthesized knowledge rather than a raw dump of daily work.
